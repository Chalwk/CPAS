## Overview

Wanaka is a small regional airport in the Upper Clutha Basin, near the southern end of Lake Wanaka. It is a base for scenic helicopter and fixed-wing operations into Mount Aspiring National Park.

## Elevation

1,142 ft AMSL.

## Local Procedures

- **Part-time tower:** Check NOTAMs.
- **CTR:** Class D when tower is active.
- High scenic traffic.

## Hazards

- Glider activity (Omarama nearby).
- Skydiving.
- Alpine weather change.

## Recommended Approaches

- **RNAV 29:** Preferred IFR.
- **Visual 11:** From the north, over the lake.
