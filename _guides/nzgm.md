Greymouth is a small regional aerodrome on the West Coast, serving the Grey District and the Paparoa National Park.

## Local Procedures

- **Coastal VFR:** Follow SH6 for the standard coastal corridor.

## Hazards

- Coastal fog and low cloud.
- Strong westerly wind.
- Paparoa National Park low-level restrictions.

## Recommended Approaches

- **Visual 01:** Straight-in from the south, over the Grey River mouth.
- **Visual 19:** Coastal, from the north.
