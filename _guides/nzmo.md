## Overview

Te Anau / Manapouri is the primary aerodrome for Fiordland, sitting on the eastern shore of Lake Manapouri. It is the last fuel stop before Milford Sound.

## Elevation

770 ft AMSL.

## Local Procedures

- Fiordland National Park overflight rules apply.
- Mountain radio 130.6 for area broadcasts.

## Hazards

- Fiordland weather can close the route in minutes.
- Lake outflow winds.
- Terrain to the north and west.

## Recommended Approaches

- **Visual 32:** From the south, over the lake.
- **Visual 14:** Straight-in from the north.
