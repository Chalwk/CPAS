## Overview

Omarama is a small aerodrome at the southern end of the Mackenzie Basin, world-famous for gliding. The field is in Class G airspace with no ATC, but glider activity can be intense.

## Elevation

1,380 ft AMSL.

## Local Procedures

- Glider winch launching common - check windsock before crossing the field.
- One-way valley operations to the north.

## Hazards

- Gliders and winch cables.
- Strong nor'westerly through the basin.

## Recommended Approaches

- **Visual 09:** From the west.
