## Overview

Haast Pass (563 m / 1,847 ft) is the lowest of the three main alpine crossings (with Arthur's and Lewis). It links the West Coast at Haast with the upper Clutha valley at [Wanaka (NZWF)](/guides/nzwf/) and [Queenstown (NZQN)](/guides/nzqn/).

## Route

1. Depart from [Hokitika (NZHK)](/guides/nzhk/) or Franz Josef / Fox for the West Coast leg.
2. Track south along the coast to **Haast**.
3. Turn east into the **Haast River** valley.
4. Follow the valley through **Haast Pass**, crossing at a minimum of **6,500 ft MSL**.
5. Descend into the **Makarora / Lake Wanaka** system.
6. Continue east to [Wanaka (NZWF)](/guides/nzwf/) or turn south for [Queenstown (NZQN)](/guides/nzqn/).

## Reporting Points

- **Haast township** - coastal junction.
- **Gates of Haast** - narrow section of the pass road.
- **Haast Pass summit** - saddle.
- **Makarora** - eastern side of the divide.
- **Lake Wanaka** - arrival into the Wanaka basin.

## Minimum Altitudes

- **6,500 ft MSL** over the pass.
- **2,000 ft AGL** minimum over Mount Aspiring National Park.
- **9,500 ft MSL** recommended for Queenstown crossing when weather permits.

## Hazards

- Narrow valley with limited go-around options.
- Very high annual rainfall - cloud and rain common.
- Mountain wave in strong westerly.
- Terrain on both sides of the pass.

## Tips

- Give yourself plenty of altitude over the pass - you lose it quickly entering the eastern valleys.
- Check the Mount Aspiring National Park overflight rules before departure.
