## Overview

Dunedin International sits on the Taieri Plain, 15 NM south-west of Dunedin city. It is the main gateway to Otago and the lower South Island.

## Elevation

4 ft AMSL.

## Local Procedures

- **CTR:** Class C. Transitions require clearance.
- Taieri (NZTI) is nearby and shares some airspace.

## Hazards

- Coastal fog - the notorious "Dunedin drizzle".
- Strong south-westerly.

## Recommended Approaches

- **ILS 03 / 21:** Preferred IFR.
- **Visual:** Straight-in over the Taieri Plain.
