## Overview

The Franz Josef and Fox glaciers descend from the Southern Alps almost to sea level on the West Coast. Their valleys are the most popular scenic corridors in the country and are flown by helicopter and fixed-wing operations daily.

{% include region_map.html %}

## Route

1. Depart [Hokitika (NZHK)](/guides/nzhk/) or the glacier fields directly.
2. Track south along the coast past **Whataroa**.
3. Turn inland into the **Waiho Valley** for Franz Josef, or the **Fox Valley** for Fox Glacier.
4. Climb into the glacier valley to view the icefall.
5. Optional glacier landing (helicopters only) - check conditions.

## Reporting Points

- **Hokitika** - coastal departure.
- **Whataroa** - coastal township, continue south toward the glacier valleys.
- **Franz Josef township** - tourist hub, helicopter base.
- **Fox Glacier township** - southern glacier base.
- **Glacier terminal faces** - the icefall viewpoints.

## Minimum Altitudes

- **2,000 ft AGL** minimum over national park land.
- **3,000 ft** minimum near Fox township. Do not overfly the towns.
- **500 ft AGL** over the coast only where permitted.
- For glacier landings: check current snow/ice conditions and DO NOT land without proper training.

## Hazards

- **Katabatic winds** - cold air flows down the glaciers and can produce sudden downdrafts.
- Glacier surfaces are unstable - never land on a glacier without special training.
- Heavy helicopter traffic in summer.
- Rapid cloud build-up - the valleys can close in minutes.

## Tips

- Do not commit to a valley unless you have a clear escape route back out.
- Monitor 118.6 for other operators' position reports.
- If weather is marginal, stay coastal and view the glaciers from the seaward end.
