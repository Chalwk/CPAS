## Overview

Kaikoura is a small coastal aerodrome on the east coast of the South Island, nestled between the Seaward Kaikoura Range and the Pacific Ocean. It is renowned for marine wildlife and dramatic mountain scenery.

## Local Procedures

- **Left-hand circuits only.**
- **Overhead join advised.**
- **Whale-watch MOU:** Private pilots must be briefed by Wings Over Whales or Air Kaikōura Aero Club.

## Hazards

- Sea breeze.
- Mountain wave off the Seawards.

## Recommended Approaches

- **Visual 23:** From the north-east, off the sea.
