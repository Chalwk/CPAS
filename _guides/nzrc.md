## Overview

Ryan's Creek is the only airfield on Stewart Island / Rakiura, near the town of Oban. The strip is unattended and requires careful planning due to its remote location.

## Local Procedures

- Unattended - broadcast intentions on CTAF.
- No fuel, no services.
- Foveaux Strait crossing requires careful weather planning.

## Hazards

- Rapid weather change across Foveaux Strait.
- Limited diversion options.
- Island wind and terrain.

## Recommended Approaches

- **Visual 04:** Over the strait, straight-in.
