## Overview

Pukaki is a small grass strip on the southern shore of the glacial Lake Pukaki. It is a useful alternative or stopover on the route into Mount Cook.

## Elevation

1,560 ft AMSL.

## Local Procedures

- Alpine conditions; watch for glider activity.

## Hazards

- Lake outflow winds.
- Mountain terrain close to the north.

## Recommended Approaches

- **Visual 09:** From the east, over the lake.
