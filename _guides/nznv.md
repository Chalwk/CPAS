## Overview

Invercargill is the southernmost city airport in New Zealand, serving Southland and as the staging point for Stewart Island and southern Fiordland.

## Elevation

5 ft AMSL.

## Local Procedures

- **CTR:** Class C. Transitions require clearance.
- Standard route to Stewart Island via the Foveaux Strait.

## Hazards

- Strong south-westerly.
- Coastal fog.
- Foveaux Strait weather.

## Recommended Approaches

- **ILS 04 / 22:** Preferred IFR.
- **Visual:** Straight-in.
