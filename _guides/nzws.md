## Overview

Westport sits on the Buller River mouth on the northern West Coast. It's a small regional aerodrome with a single sealed runway and a challenging coastal weather environment.

## Elevation

13 ft AMSL.

## Local Procedures

- Check NOTAMs for tower hours; often AFIS or CTAF.
- Coastal VFR routes along the shoreline are common.

## Hazards

- Strong westerly and south-westerly winds off the Tasman Sea.
- Low cloud and drizzle common.
- Buller Gorge outflow winds.

## Recommended Approaches

- **RNAV 04 / 22:** Preferred IFR.
- **Visual:** Coastal arrival from Hokitika, or over the Buller River from the east.
