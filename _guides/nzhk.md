## Overview

Hokitika is the main West Coast airport, sitting between the Tasman Sea and the foothills of the Southern Alps. It is the natural arrival point for glacier country and Arthur's Pass crossings.

## Elevation

153 ft AMSL.

## Local Procedures

- **Unattended:** No tower or ATIS - broadcast on CTAF 119.1.
- **Approaches:** RNAV (GNSS) and NDB/DME approaches available.
- **Coastal corridor:** Class G below 1500 ft.

## Hazards

- Frequent low cloud and heavy West Coast rain.
- Strong coastal wind and sea breeze convergence.
- Helicopter traffic to the glaciers (Franz Josef / Fox).

## Recommended Approaches

- **RNAV 21:** Straight-in from the north.
- **Visual 03:** Coastal arrival, watch for terrain to the east.
